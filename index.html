Citizen Rights & Counter-Fraud Guidelines

Safeguard your documents and avoid paying unauthorized commissions

01

Never Hand Over Original CNIC to Touts

Official registration centers will only view originals on desk for verification and scan copies. Never surrender originals to unauthorized agents outside offices.

02

Verify Electronic PSID Payments

All modern state fees (Passports, e-Challan, FBR) now support direct 1Link/e-Pay digital bank transfers. Always pay directly from your banking app using standard PSID numbers.

03

Insist on Official Machine Receipts

Ensure every token, form submission, and fee deposit provides a computerized computer-printed tracking receipt with a timestamp and official serial number.
