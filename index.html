Citizen Protection & Counter-Fraud Advisory

Important official rules to keep your money and identity safe

RULE 01

Never Pay Outside Agents

All fees must be deposited directly at bank counters, NADRA e-Sahulat, or through official 1Link/e-Pay digital channels. Agents standing outside have no legal authority.

RULE 02

Keep Original Documents Safe

Government officers only inspect original documents at the counter for immediate visual verification. Never leave original cards with anyone.

RULE 03

Always Take Computerized Receipts

Every valid token, fee deposit, or application provides an official printed tracking receipt. Always verify the tracking number.
